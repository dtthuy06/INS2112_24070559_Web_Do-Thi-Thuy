Welcome to CodeBreakers Club

We are a student club for anyone who wants to learn web development. No experience needed: you start with a blank file, and by the end of the semester you ship a real website. Curious? Get in touch and come to our next session.

What We Do

  • Weekly workshops on HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • Pair-programming sessions where beginners and seniors build together
  • Monthly mini-hackathons with small prizes
  • Mock interviews and portfolio reviews

Why Join Us?

Learn by building

Every session ends with something that runs in your browser.

Get unstuck fast

Mentors are in the room, so a bug rarely costs you more than ten minutes.

Build a portfolio

You leave with projects you can show to employers.
