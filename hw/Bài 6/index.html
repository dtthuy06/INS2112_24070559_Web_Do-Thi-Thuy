Welcome to CodeBreakers Club

We are a student club for anyone who wants to learn web development. No experience needed: you start with a blank file, and by the end of the semester you ship a real website.

What We Do

  • Weekly workshops on HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • Pair-programming sessions where beginners and seniors build together
  • Monthly mini-hackathons with small prizes
